Oreo Rebrand

This project features redesigns of many pages of the Oreo brand website.

Redesign 1 Redesign 2 Redesign 3 Redesign 4

Tech Stack

Figma

Inspiration

As part of my MEJO 581 class, I was tasked with picking a brand to redesign their website pages for utilizing their brand style guide. I decided to redesign for Oreo as the creativity of their website seemed interesting to redesign for.

Brainstorming

Utilizing the Mondelez Internation brand guidelines, the style guide that Oreo follows, I settles on the same blue color that is used across their website. I also wanted to utilize a similar white font and style that they used. I wanted to ensure consistency with their fonts, colors, and branding guidelines by also copying the navigation and footer bars. All of the characters in headings and paragraphs were all capitalized.

Development

Each week of the class, I redesigned different pages of the Oreo website in Figma. These included a 404 page, a countdown page, a loading page, and a feedback popup page. The images were generated using ChatGPT.

Result of Project

This project allowed me to get more comfortable with Figma, utilizing different elements and features. Each redesign page features a sleek, aesthetic, and brand accurate style.
