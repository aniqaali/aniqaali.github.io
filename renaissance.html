Italian Renaissance Art and Artists

An informative Figma design informing users of 3 specific Italian Renaissance painters and their works.

View Project

Tech Stack

Figma

Inspiration

As someone who enjoys painting and reading up on history, I wanted to combine these two in a project, thus resulting in a focus on the Italian Renaissance. This design was curated to allow users to dive into the Renaissance period through interactives and visual aesthetics.

Brainstorming

I researched which colors,fonts, and audio would be most effective in immersing the audience into the Renaissance time period. I chose beige, light yellow, brown, and black for most of the website layout. To add to the theme, I also wanted to incorporate scrolls and light sketches to create less of a modern look.

Development

I utilized light colors for the background, with darker colors for the fonts. The font reflects handwritten text to represent writing on scrolls with ink. I also asked AI to create hand drawn sketch type drawings. I added interactives on a map to click through the different pages of the design. Many of the elements were created in Figma while others were gathered from the internet.

Result of Project

The final design successfully immerses users in the Italian Renaissance period through its visual aesthetics and interactive elements. The use of historical colors and typography creates an authentic atmosphere, while the interactive map allows for seamless navigation between different sections of the design.

Future Recommendations

Future improvements could include adding more interactive elements, expanding the content to include more artists and artworks, and improving the overall user experience.
