Mealify

Mealify is a food and nutrition app concept designed to help users search recipes, explore ingredients, and build healthier meal plans.

View Project

Add main project screenshot here

Tech Stack

Swift SwiftUI Spoonacular API

The Problem

Many college students want to eat healthier, but finding affordable meals, understanding nutrition, and keeping track of food choices can feel overwhelming. Mealify was designed to make food discovery more accessible, visual, and simple.

Brainstorming

During the brainstorming stage, I explored how users might search for recipes, save ingredients, and organize meals throughout the day. I focused on creating a design that felt friendly, easy to navigate, and useful for students with busy schedules.

Final Solution & Development

The final solution included recipe search, ingredient information, nutrition details, and a lunchbox-style saving feature. I used SwiftUI to build the interface and connected the app to the Spoonacular API to retrieve recipe and food data.

Result of Project

Mealify became a functional app prototype that combined user-centered design with API-based development. Through this project, I strengthened my skills in SwiftUI, working with external APIs, structuring app navigation, and designing for real user needs.

Future Recommendations

Future improvements could include user accounts, grocery price comparisons, daily calorie and protein goals, saved meal folders, and personalized recipe suggestions.
