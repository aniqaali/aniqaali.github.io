Cloudnine

Cloudnine is a music festival website designed to ensure users have an efficient and fun digital experience.

View Project

Tech Stack

Figma

The Problem

Many music festival attendees utilize the websites and apps for these music festivals to gain information about tickets, prices, festival ground layouts and artist lineups. However, navigating these websites can often be complicated and time consuming. Often times, there are not digital maps for users to view neither are there more efficient ways for users to remember their itinerary for the day than setting a screenshot of a schedule for the day as their phone lockscreen. I wanted to create the digital music festival experience for music festival attendees more efficient and seamless.

Brainstorming

Initially, I created a "How might we?" question and narrowed down my audience by researching the age range of people that attended music festivals and those who were most adept with technology. Then, I interviewed 2 music festival website users who fell under the category of my audience. I asked them a series of 10 research questions and used my findings to create user stories, wireframes, and user journey maps. I learned that many users struggle to navigate the festival grounds and struggle with the nested nature of many of these music festival websites.The main foci are the interactive map and the schedule builder. For my styling choices, the theme I chose is Y2K and 70s, so I went with pastel colors, bubbly fonts, and colorful imagery. I took inspiration from the websites of the following music festivals: Breakaway, Coachella, Mempho Fest, LollaPalooza.

Final Solution & Development

The final product is a fully functional Figma music festival website design called CloudNine. I designed each of the pages and elements while incorporating some imagery from ChatGPT and the internet. The interactive map includes a map key with iconography representing where stages, food, and merchandise are located. Each stage can be clicked on to reveal the list of artists performming at that location with dates and times. The schedule builder allows users to build their daily schedule by adding the artists they would like to see for each day on the website itself. To ensure efficiency, accessibility, ease of use, and flow of the website, live user testing was conducted through the User Testing website. Feeback given through this testing was further incorporated to improve the design.

Result of Project

Cloudnine allowed me to fully immerse myself in industry level user testing, UI/UX research, and website development. I learned how to ensure that websites truly cater to users and answer research problems without incorporating biases of what I personally think would be useful or visually appealing to the user.

Future Recommendations

Future improvements could include live map navigation and location tracking of friends or family also at the festival.
